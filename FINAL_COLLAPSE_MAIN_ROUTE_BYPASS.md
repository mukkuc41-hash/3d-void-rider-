# Final Collapse Main-Route Bypass

In Black Hole Mode > Submode 10 (Final Collapse), completing two full laps unlocks a main-route bypass for lap 3. At the terminal fork, the player can keep driving straight without selecting Routes 01–03. The game only enters one of those branches when the player explicitly selects it. The lap-3 HAVEN-04 spur remains a separate option at its own approach and retains its existing unlock conditions.

The bypass also disables the low-speed hold at the main fork so the ship can continue forward. This change is scoped to Final Collapse and does not change Modes 01 or other modes.
