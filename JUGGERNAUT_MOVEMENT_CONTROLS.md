# Juggernaut / Titan Boss Combined Controls

## One combined mobile controller
- Forward / Backward: thrust along the ship's facing direction.
- Left / Right: lateral strafe.
- Up / Down (Altitude): vertical movement independent of aiming.
- Analog flight joystick: smooth steering and pitch.
- Boost, fire, and ability buttons remain independent so they can be used while moving.

## Ability-strength tuning
- Shockwave: increased damage and outward force.
- Bulwark: increased shield restoration.
- Warp Dash: increased forward burst.

Desktop keyboard controls remain available. Vertical thrust is currently provided by the combined on-screen controller.
