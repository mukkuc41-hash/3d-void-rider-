# Spherical Cargo Docking Dome — Convoy Guardian SAVIOUR

- Extends the convoy route into a large spherical hangar at the destination.
- Adds a transparent/wireframe dome, illuminated landing pads, four parking bays, sensor hoops, segmented chamber gates, and mechanical parking clamps.
- At the end of SAVIOUR, the mission enters a timed, sequential docking phase. Each surviving vessel approaches its assigned bay, the sensor gate opens, the ship settles into its parking position, and the locks engage before the next vessel is admitted.
- The current convoy has one lead flagship and three cargo ships; all surviving ships dock one at a time. The three cargo vessels occupy three of the four marked bays.
- Victory/results are triggered only after the surviving convoy ships have completed the parking sequence.

Validation: TypeScript transpile syntax checks and ZIP integrity check performed. Full Vite build/runtime validation requires the project's dependencies to be installed.
